MY PROCESS

Explore broadly.
Move forward with purpose.

Research, direction, design and delivery. Connected by evidence, shaped with the team.

01 / Understand

Find the problem behind the request.

Research the experience with users. Map the journey, listen to stakeholders and challenge the assumptions shaping the brief.

Journey mapping · Research · Opportunity framing

What moves forwardShared evidence

02 / Set direction

Turn evidence into a clear product direction.

Bring product, design and engineering around the priorities. Make the trade-offs explicit and define what success needs to look like.

Product direction · Prioritisation · Success criteria

What moves forwardA focused brief

03 / Make & test

Make the options tangible. Test the decisions.

Explore journeys, interactions and prototypes. Put alternatives in front of users, learn what works and refine the details that matter.

Interaction design · Prototyping · User validation

What moves forwardA tested approach

04 / Deliver

Carry the quality into the product.

Build reusable patterns and clear interaction rules. Work with engineering through implementation, review and the next iteration.

Design systems · Implementation review · Iteration
