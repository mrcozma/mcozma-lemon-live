MY PROCESS

A shared direction.
Room to learn.

The sequence gives the work structure. New evidence can take us back a step.

  1. 01

    Align

    What are we trying to change?

    Bring the team together around the problem, constraints and what success should mean.

    Shared direction
  2. 02

    Explore

    What do we need to understand?

    Connect research, conversations and the existing experience. Make assumptions visible.

    A clearer problem
  3. 03

    Design

    What can we make to learn?

    Turn ideas into flows and prototypes. Test important assumptions and refine the detail.

    Evidence to decide
  4. 04

    Deliver

    How will it work in practice?

    Partner with engineering through implementation, feedback and the next iteration.

    Intent into experience

↶ Revisit the question as the evidence changes.
